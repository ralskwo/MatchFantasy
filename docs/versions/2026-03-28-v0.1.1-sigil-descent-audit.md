## Key Findings Captured

- `sigil_descent` has a stronger separation between battle logic and battle presentation than the current `MatchFantasy` baseline.
- The best upgrade candidates are presentation planning, projectile sequencing, special-tile combo expansion, reward metadata, and battle snapshot persistence.
- The least portable pieces are the single-controller app flow and the all-widget battle renderer.
- The local `sigil_descent` copy could not be validated on this machine because it requires Dart `^3.11.1`, while the available SDK is `3.8.1`.
